# Aircraft pack references and scope

First edition, 12 October 2026. Both miniatures share the carrier's 1:400 scale.

## MiG-29K

Nominal design baseline: 17.3 m length and 11.99 m spread span, yielding 43.25 × 29.975 mm. Published figures vary by version and measurement conventions. This is an approximate craft baseline, not a measured three-view reconstruction.

The [Rosoboronexport product PDF](https://roe.ru/pdfs/pdf_4664.pdf) was found in search but its full contents could not be retrieved in this session; do not treat it as verified support for a particular dimension. The [Indian government announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/erelcontent.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=98416) supports the type's association with Vikramaditya.

The first pattern uses one flat wing/tailplane outline, one open-bottom folded body and two fins with glue feet. Wings are spread only; no landing gear, stores or movable parts. Six sets on sheet 1.

## Mil Mi-24

[Vertical Flight Society / Vertipedia](https://vertipedia.vtol.org/aircraft/getAircraft/aircraftID/478) lists a 17.50 m airframe length, 17.30 m main rotor diameter, 6.65 m airframe width, five main blades and three tail blades. At 1:400 these become a 43.75 mm fuselage, a 43.25 mm swept main-rotor diameter and 16.625 mm stub-wing span. A five-blade rotor's bounding box is smaller than its swept diameter.

The [Czech Ministry of Defence overview](https://acr.mo.gov.cz/scripts/detail.php?id=6298) also lists the 17.3 m main rotor diameter.

The original paper pattern is a generic tandem-cockpit Hind approximation, not a variant-specific replica. Tail rotor size, roof width, body depth, tailplane and blade widths are modelling choices. The open-bottom body uses a roof to connect two printed sides. Separate stub wings use folded root tabs; the rotor mast is an open-bottom folded cap. Seven pieces per helicopter, two sets on sheet 2. No landing gear or spinning rotors.

The Mi-24 is the user's custom display addition, not an asserted Vikramaditya operating aircraft. Six fighters plus two helicopters is a craft pack quantity, not a claimed operational air-wing composition.

## Validation limits

The generator checks page size, margins, inter-piece bounding-box separation, and main scale dimensions. Digital checks do not establish physical fit, glue strength, cutting practicality or paper-thickness tolerance. Print one set first and revise after assembly feedback.
