# Aircraft shape-study references

## Evidence inspected

- [US Army Hind side profile, via Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mil_Mi-24_1-view_line_drawing.svg). Public-domain recognition illustration; original US Government work, SVG conversion by Malyszkz and extraction by Mikhail Ryazanov. Used to inspect cockpit steps, cabin/tail proportions and the swept tail-fin arrangement. Recognition art is not a production drawing.
- [Indian Navy MiG-29K photograph, 14 June 2014](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:A_MiG-29K_performs_a_touch_and_go_landing_on_INS_Vikramaditya_during_Narendra_Modi%27s_visit.jpg). Government Open Data License–India. Used to inspect the radome, canopy, wing-root blending, engine placement and twin tail. Perspective photographs do not define exact cross-sections.
- [Vertical Flight Society / Vertipedia](https://vertipedia.vtol.org/aircraft/getAircraft/aircraftID/478): Mi-24 17.50 m airframe length, 17.30 m main rotor diameter, 6.65 m airframe width, five main and three tail blades. At 1:400: 43.75 mm airframe, 43.25 mm swept rotor diameter, 16.625 mm stub-wing span. Tail rotor tips extend beyond the airframe measurement.
- [Indian government aircraft announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/erelcontent.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=98416) supports the MiG-29K/Vikramaditya association.

The public Army manual hosted on marines.mil returned 403. A general MiG-29 drawing was also located, but was not treated as a verified MiG-29K blueprint. No production blueprint or measured cross-section set was obtained.

## Modelling decisions

MiG-29K nominal baseline remains 17.3 m long and 11.99 m spread span: 43.25 × 29.975 mm. Published dimensions differ by version/measurement convention. Elliptical body stations, engine radii, canopy stations, wing roots and fin angles are manually inferred modelling choices, not traced or certified dimensions.

The Hind uses nine connected body skins to establish stepped glazing, cabin volume and a narrow rising boom, with two rounded engine fairings, separate drooping wings and a swept tail fin. MiG parts include six fuselage skins, two wings, six engine-pod skins, two stabilizers, two fins and two half-shell canopy skins. Paint and markings are illustrative rather than airframe-specific. Small external equipment, gear, detailed intakes and rotor mechanics are omitted.

## Construction and limits

Every shell uses triangulated eight-sided rings. The generator unfolds each triangle preserving its edge lengths; adjoining rings share coordinates. Underbody seam tabs and front joining tabs are included. Canopy halves remain open underneath. Flat wing/fin roots use bent glue feet and small glue fillets on curved surfaces.

Digital validation covers A4 size, margins, hinge connectivity, triangle lengths and shell/tab overlap. It does not prove physical fit, shape accuracy against a dimensional survey, paper-thickness tolerance or practical assembly. At 1:400 many tabs are below 1 mm. Test one before assembling the complete six-fighter/two-helicopter display pack.

Mi-24 is a user-selected custom display addition, not a claimed Vikramaditya air-group aircraft. The six/two pack quantity is a craft layout, not an operational loadout.
