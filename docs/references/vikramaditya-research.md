# INS Vikramaditya reference research

Research date: 12 October 2026. Purpose: improve the exterior fidelity of the printable paper model. No verified shipyard construction blueprint or dimensioned general-arrangement set was located in this search. Public drawings below must not be represented as official plans.

## Sources and what they establish

1. Ministry of Defence commissioning release (16 November 2013): https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/PrintRelease.aspx?lang=3&reg=3&relid=100633
   Confirms approximately 284 m overall length and 60 m maximum beam. These support overall scale only, not individual deck, hull or island dimensions. In particular, maximum beam is not automatically the width of the flight-deck polygon or waterline hull.
2. Ministry of Defence photograph, recorded as 3 May 2020: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:INS_Vikramaditya_with_full_air_asset_loadout.jpg
   Primary visual evidence for the completed vessel. Commons identifies the Ministry of Defence as author and links the original DefPRO Mumbai source. Use visible exterior features; an oblique photograph is not an orthographic measurement drawing. Check the file page's GODL attribution terms before redistributing an image.
3. Chanakyathegreat's original graphical illustration (22 October 2009): https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:INS_Vikramaditya_cg.png
   Artist-authored reference, not a shipyard blueprint. Predates commissioning. Listed under CC BY-SA 3.0; derivative reuse requires applicable attribution and share-alike treatment. Do not treat its geometry as verified dimensions.
4. Vitaly Kuzmin photograph of the IMDS-2007 exhibition model: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Model_of_INS_%22Vikramaditya%22.jpg
   Primary photograph of a model, not the completed ship. Useful for interpreting shapes, subject to later photographic checks. Predates commissioning; exhibition details may differ. File page describes CC BY-SA licensing.
5. AllDrawings listing: https://alldrawings.ru/en/pictures/item/ship-ins-vikramaditya-aircraft-carrier-ex-ussr-kiev-drawings-dimensions-pictures
   Unverified lead only. Advertises a 99 KB drawing archive; original image request timed out and the download page did not expose a verified drawing in this session. Provenance, precision and reuse rights are unestablished. Do not use for dimensions yet.
6. TCET NCC's model-building project: https://ncc.tcetmumbai.in/ins-vikramaditya.html
   First-party account of a December 2025–January 2026 model project mentioning blueprints. The retrieved page does not provide a verified downloadable plan set. A possible future source enquiry, not measurement evidence. No contact was made.

## Accuracy-first next pass

- Choose a documented appearance/date before combining sources. Use completed-ship photographs as the main reference, and keep older conceptual drawings secondary.
- Build top, port, starboard, bow and stern reference views. Record each dimension as published, estimated from imagery, or simplified for assembly. Never imply sub-millimetre real-ship accuracy from low-resolution pictures.
- Correct the flight-deck perimeter, landing-strip angle, lift positions and island footprint. Replace schematic marks only where the evidence supports the replacement.
- Replace the rectangular hull approximation with several shaped sections and an internal spine/bulkhead structure. Keep deck and hull widths separate.
- Develop a curved ski-jump paper skin over ribs; the current straight wedge is a deliberate simplification. Verify the profile before assigning dimensions.
- Refine stepped bridge volumes, funnel shape, mast silhouette, visible platforms and deck-edge sponsons. Current repeated windows, doors, hull portholes and radar grids are schematic artwork, not verified replicas.
- Offer optional small components such as boats, life-raft canisters and railings where photographs establish their placement. At small scale, some features should remain printed.
- Add aircraft only after the deck geometry is stable; aircraft must use the same nominal scale.
- For every geometry change regenerate cut nets, glue tabs, part IDs, previews and assembly instructions, then make a physical prototype.

## Scale options for paper construction

Using the published approximate 284 m overall length:

| Scale | Model length | Construction implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1:700 (previous) | 40.6 cm | Compact; many small details remain printed. |
| 1:500 | 56.8 cm | More room for separate folded components; more A4 sections. |
| 1:400 (selected) | 71.0 cm | Better candidate for a highly detailed model and small aircraft; needs a stronger internal structure. |

A4 specifies sheet size, not finished model size. Large models can be tiled across sheets. The user selected the 71 cm, 1:400 option on 12 October 2026. Version 0.3 implements that size with four hull/deck sections and internal supports; the exterior geometry is still schematic.
